Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 16, 2026

Welcome to the Fourth Scholarship mobile application. Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect the absolute minimum personal information required to provide our educational services. This includes:

  • Basic Student Details: Name, Mobile Number, and School Name.
  • Educational Progress: Quiz scores and exam attempt metrics.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information collected is used solely for the following purposes:

  • To track student learning progress and generate educational assessments.
  • To assist students in preparing for their scholarship exams effectively.
  • To maintain the core functionality of the learning platform.

3. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We respect your privacy completely. No personal data is shared, sold, rented, or disclosed to any third parties under any circumstances.

4. Data Security

We implement strict operational and technical measures to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. All data remains securely processed within our system infrastructure.

5. Your Rights and Data Deletion

You retain full control over your information. Users can request the permanent deletion of their account and all associated personal records at any time. To request data deletion, please contact us through the application or via our support channels.

6. Compliance

This application strives to adhere strictly to mobile platform family safety standards, ensuring a safe, encrypted environment tailored for educational enhancement.
